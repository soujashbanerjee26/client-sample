Scene 04 — Process

How A Project Runs

Four stages, in order — same pipeline whether it's a 15-second reel or a 3-minute film.

01

Ingest & Brief

Footage in, brief locked. Every clip gets logged before I touch a timeline.

02

Assembly Cut

Story first, polish later. A rough cut that proves the edit works before it looks good.

03

Fine Cut & Grade

Trimmed to the frame, graded for mood, sound designed to sit under the picture.

04

Deliver & Master

Exported for every platform it needs to live on. Masters archived, always.
